What I do

Helping you move forward as yourself

Be your most effective self

Not a version of yourself shaped by other people's expectations, but the one that fits who you are and what you want. We get clear on how you make decisions, where your energy goes, what's working, and what quietly isn't. Then we build practical habits and mindsets so you show up that way more often, with less effort and less second-guessing.

Lead in your own style

There is no single right way to lead. The most effective leaders know their strengths, name their values, and lead from there. Whether you're stepping into a formal leadership role for the first time or want to bring more of yourself to your teams and relationships, we'll uncover the style that's yours and practice it until it feels natural.

Navigate life transitions

For women in the moments when the ground shifts: moving somewhere very different and rebuilding a sense of home, a career change chosen or unexpected, becoming a caregiver, or a change in dreams you once counted on. I help you sort through what's ending and what's beginning, and move forward with intention.

Who I work with

You might recognize yourself here

My clients arrive at many different moments, but they share a feeling: something needs to change, and they want to approach it thoughtfully.

You're feeling stuck

In your career or in your life. You want something different but haven't named it yet, or you're standing at a fork with several paths and no certainty about which to take.

Your life is changing

A move, a new role, a restructuring at work, an empty nest, a health event, a relationship change. Some changes we choose and some choose us. Coaching gives you a place to process, adapt deliberately, and regain agency over how you respond.

You want to show up better

For yourself, your family, your friends, or your team. Communicate more clearly, set healthier boundaries, be more present, bring confidence to difficult conversations, or stop running on autopilot.

You're stepping into leadership

You want to lead in a way that honors your values, without imitating someone else. You want to lead as yourself: credible, grounded, and clear about what you stand for.

The questions my clients bring

What comes next for me?

In your career, your personal life, or this new chapter: what do you actually want, and what's the first honest step toward it?

How do I adapt to what's changing?

How do you move through this transition without losing yourself in it, and maybe come out more grounded than before?

How do I find my own clarity and confidence?

Not the borrowed kind that fades when things get hard, but the kind built on knowing yourself and trusting your own judgment.

What makes coaching work

A partnership, with two conditions

An openness to explore

The most valuable insights come from looking honestly at what you believe about yourself and what you want, including the beliefs that have been quietly running the show. Curiosity, not judgment, is the tool we use.

A commitment to the work

Real change happens between sessions as much as in them. My clients come prepared to reflect, try things, notice what happens, and keep at it. I bring the structure, the questions, and the accountability. You bring your life, honestly.
